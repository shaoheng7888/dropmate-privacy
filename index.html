DropMate 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年8月2日

欢迎使用 DropMate（以下简称「本应用」）。我们非常重视您的隐私。本隐私政策旨在说明本应用如何处理您的数据。

一、我们收集的数据

本应用不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人身份信息，包括但不限于您的姓名、邮箱地址、电话号码、位置信息等。

本应用的所有功能（文件收纳、视频桌面背景、窗口管理等）均在您的设备本地完成，不需要联网访问您的个人资料。

二、文件数据

  • 您通过本应用收纳的文件，仅存储在您设备的本地存储目录中。
  • 本应用不会将您的任何文件上传至任何服务器，也不会修改、删除或复制您的原始文件。
  • 您移除文件时，文件将返回其原始存放位置。

三、视频内容

本应用提供的视频壁纸素材托管于内容分发网络（CDN），仅用于展示视频内容。我们不会利用这些访问获取您的个人数据。

四、App 内购买

本应用的「全能版」通过 Apple 的 App 内购买（In-App Purchase, IAP）提供。购买行为及支付信息由 Apple 处理，我们不会获取您的支付账号、银行卡等敏感信息。

五、第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方广告、统计或追踪 SDK。我们不会将您的数据共享、出售或出租给任何第三方。

六、数据安全

由于本应用不收集数据，因此不存在个人数据泄露的风险。您的所有内容均保存在您自己的设备上。

七、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会收集任何儿童的个人信息。

八、政策变更

如本隐私政策发生任何变更，我们将在本页面更新。变更后的政策自发布之日起生效。

DropMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Welcome to DropMate (the "App"). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. Data We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or upload any personally identifiable information, including but not limited to your name, email address, phone number, or location.

All features of the App (file organization, video desktop backgrounds, window management, etc.) work locally on your device and do not require internet access to your personal data.

2. File Data

  • Files you store in the App are kept only in a local storage directory on your device.
  • The App does not upload any of your files to any server, nor does it modify, delete, or copy your original files.
  • When you remove a file, it returns to its original location.

3. Video Content

The video wallpaper assets provided by the App are hosted on a Content Delivery Network (CDN) for display purposes only. We do not use such access to obtain your personal data.

4. In-App Purchases

The "Pro" version of the App is provided through Apple's In-App Purchase (IAP). Purchases and payment information are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment account or bank card information.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not share, sell, or rent your data to any third party.

6. Data Security

Because the App does not collect any data, there is no risk of personal data leakage. All of your content stays on your own device.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

8. Policy Changes

If this Privacy Policy changes, we will update this page. The revised policy takes effect upon publication.
